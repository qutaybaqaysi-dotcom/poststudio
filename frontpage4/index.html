1. Each morning at 6:41 New York time, the front pages of selected newspapers are captured.
  2. A code removes the text and keeps the lines, the colors and parts of the pictures.
  3. The day's pages are stacked here until the next capture.
  4. You may arrange them, crop them, and keep the arrangement as a code.
  5. One print is made each day, by the first person to crop and order it.
  6. If no one orders, the day closes at midnight. At 6:41 the next day's pages arrive.
  7. No more than 20 prints are made in a month.
  8. Each print is labeled with the date, the time, and the maker/buyer's name.
